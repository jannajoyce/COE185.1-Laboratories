2. (1 point) Briefly explain why updating the LCD display in your ISR could
lead to undesirable behavior for this lab.

Updating the LCD within an ISR can cause delays and missed interrupts, as ISRs should be short and efficient. LCD updates are time-consuming and can disrupt real-time operations, cause inefficient context switching, and risk data corruption if both the ISR and main program access the LCD simultaneously. For efficient handling, it's better to set a flag in the ISR and update the LCD in the main loop.
